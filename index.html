How it is calculated

Ring: Kgs/spl/day = GPSS × 3 ÷ 1000. Vortex: Kgs/spl/day = KDPS × 3.

Spinning cost = Fixed cost ÷ Kgs/spl/day. Fibre cost = sum of (price ÷ Y/R% × weight). 100% cotton rows use the master multiplier (card ×1.05, comb ×1.15) instead of Y/R%.

Margin type uses margin per month on 1000 ring spindles: Very Good above 10,000, Good above 6,000, Avg. above 3,000, Poor above 0, Negative otherwise. Margin % is on selling price. Margin / kg (TCC) = selling price − total cost. Conv. costing / kg (CC) = selling price − fibre cost.

Source: Process wise GPSS / KDPS / YR master file.

Fibre prices and fixed cost

Cotton grades are in $/lb (×2.2046 = $/kg), everything else in $/kg. Clean cost = price/kg ÷ Y/R%. Mixes with no polyester, recycled polyester or viscose use a factor instead of Y/R%: the virgin factor (1.05) when the mix is only virgin cotton, otherwise the cotton factor (1.03) for loose cotton, D1D2, noil, RBD and waste mixes. Cotton, polyester, recycled cotton and other fibre grades come from the master price list (14/10/2026); D1D2, noil, filter, hard waste, RBD and loose cotton follow the OE costing sheet (01/09/2026).

How it is calculated

TPI = √Count × TM. Kg/spl/day = 7.2 × Speed ÷ Count ÷ TPI × 0.95 ÷ 1000 × 3.

Spinning cost = Fixed cost ÷ Kg/spl/day ÷ spinning efficiency (0.94 as in the weekly factory sheet). Total = Spinning + clean fibre cost.

Margin % is on cost, as in the OE costing sheet. Margin type uses margin per month on 1000 spindles. Margin / kg (TCC) = selling price − total cost. Conv. costing / kg (CC) = selling price − fibre cost.

Mix option: the "fibre not available" conditions come from the Process wise Mixing plan remarks (issue 04); choose one and the fibre is swapped at the same %. The other options load a different standard mix, such as Loose 50 / D1D2 30 / RBD 20 or Virgin 70 / Noil 30.

Questions and answers

What is in the total cost per kg?

Clean fibre cost plus spinning cost. Clean fibre cost is each fibre’s price ÷ Y/R % × its share of the blend, added up. Spinning cost is the fixed cost ÷ kgs per spindle per day.

Rotor also divides the spinning cost by the spinning efficiency (0.94 unless a process sets another).

What does Y/R mean?

Y/R is the share of the bought fibre that ends up as yarn. At 70% Y/R, 1 kg of yarn needs about 1.43 kg of fibre, so the fibre cost is the price ÷ 0.70.

100% cotton blends skip Y/R. Ring and Vortex use the master multiplier (card ×1.05, comb ×1.15). Rotor uses the virgin factor (1.05) for virgin cotton only and the cotton factor (1.03) for other cotton-only mixes.

How is the margin type decided?

By the margin per month on 1000 spindles: Very Good above 10,000, Good above 6,000, Avg. above 3,000, Poor above 0 and Negative otherwise. Enter a selling price to see it.

What does “Use this price” do?

It fills in the lowest selling price that still gives a Very Good margin (above 10,000 a month on 1000 spindles). Fine tune it with the −0.05 and +0.05 buttons or type your own price.

Can I change the fibre prices?

Yes. Pick another grade from a fibre’s dropdown, or edit a price in the fibre prices section. Changes apply to this page only and go back to the master prices when you reload.

Is anything I type saved or sent?

No. The costing runs in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server. Only your light or night choice is remembered on this device.
